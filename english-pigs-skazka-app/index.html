Весь текст

Once upon a time there were three little pigs. Their names were Nif-Nif, Naf-Naf and Nuf-Nuf. All summer they played and had fun. But in autumn it became cold. It was time to build a house, said Naf-Naf. But the brothers did not listen to him. Nif-Nif built a house of straw. It was fast and easy. Nuf-Nuf built a house of twigs. Also very quickly. And Naf-Naf worked all day long and built a house of bricks. What a house! laughed the brothers. In the forest lived an angry hungry wolf. He found out about the pigs and came to the straw house. Open up! cried the wolf. Or I will blow and break your house. The pig did not open. The wolf blew and the straw house flew away. Nif-Nif ran to Nuf-Nuf. The wolf also came to the house of twigs. He blew and the twigs flew away. Both pigs ran to Naf-Naf. The wolf blew and blew, but the brick house did not break. Then the wolf climbed on the roof and went down the chimney. Naf-Naf quickly closed the lid of the pot. The wolf fell right into the boiling water. He jumped out and ran away into the forest. And nobody ever saw him again. And the three pigs began to live together in a strong brick house.
